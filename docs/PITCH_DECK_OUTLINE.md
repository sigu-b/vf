- No real-time monitoring → reactive maintenance
- Unexpected downtime costs $500-2000 per site/month
- Manual inspections are expensive and inefficient

### Market Impact

- Off-grid solar serves 2M+ households
- Maintenance is largest operational cost after installation

### Current Solutions

❌ Fixed cleaning schedules (inefficient)
❌ No predictive intelligence

## Slide 3: The Solution

### SPMS: AI-Driven Predictive Maintenance

**How It Works:**
1. **IoT Sensors** monitor dust (PM10/PM2.5), temperature, humidity, irradiance
2. **AI Model** predicts performance degradation in real-time
3. **Smart Alerts** recommend cleaning only when needed
4. **Dashboard** provides actionable insights to operators

**Key Innovation:**
Physics + AI hybrid approach optimized for African environmental conditions
